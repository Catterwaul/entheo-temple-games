Game Events

As discussed in-person recently, this is a proposal to our ministers for a new recurring event for Entheo Temple.

Three potential events are detailed below, presented similarly to other events on Heylo. They use games that the Catterwauls already own as a launchpad for exploring various forms of limited communication. Over time, the game selection would be tuned to best suit attending members' interests, as we get to know each other better.

Accommodations

  • Any group size. Each game seats somewhere between 4 and 9 players, so a bigger crowd can split into several tables.
  • We bring the games from our own collection, and teach each one at the table.
  • Flexible in the moment. We’ll focus on the games listed in each event, but we’re happy to teach and play other games too, based on what the guests enjoy.
  • Each event closes with a short sharing circle: what did we notice about how we connected?

When to host

Part of the question is when. We’re night owls, and later times work better on weekdays for Jessy’s work schedule (which is somewhat flexible about when it ends).

We’d like to try hosting monthly, maybe alternating between:

  • A weekday evening, roughly 6:30–10:30 PM
  • A Saturday, also a 4-hour slot, probably earlier in the day

That way, people who can’t make one can likely make the other, and we can see which works best for everyone.

Not just board games

You may have noticed from your visit that we’re what’s called “omnigamers”: we play games of every kind. The events below use board and card games, but the meetup doesn’t need to be limited to any one type of game.

For example, our living room can support a whole group playing a video game together, with everyone using their own phone as a controller. The Jackbox Party Packs have a handful of great games that work this way in a living room setting.

We’d also love to take games outside our home. Chimera Corp Saga at Escape Artist in Greenville, SC is three escape rooms played back to back, with linking storylines, bonus content throughout, and an exclusive ending: a 3+ hour adventure for 2–8 players. It’s $499 for up to 4 players, plus $124.75 for each additional player, so a full group of 8 works out to $124.75 each. We know the owners personally and would love to support them by bringing a group. We’ve already played these rooms (and all their others) individually, so we’d be happy to act as observers and help anyone who gets stuck, rather than solving them ourselves again.

A name for the meetup

We’d love for you to choose the name. Since nobody else is hosting games, we think “game” belongs in it, but not “board game,” since there may not always be boards. Some ideas:

  • Games of Oneness: ties directly to “we are all One”
  • Connection Games: fits the communication theme
  • Game Circle: matches the Temple’s “circle” language, though it could blur with Breathwork and Gratitude Circles

What we need from you

  • A name for the meetup
  • Which event to start with
  • Which schedule works best (see “When to host” above)
  • A venue: our home, a member’s home, or alongside an existing gathering
  • A suggested donation range, if any
  • Whether children are welcome
  • Any changes to the listings below

We’ve seen sections like What to Bring, a first-time connection call, a suggested donation, and a sign-off on the announcement posts for other events. We’d also like your input on whether ours should include a timeline. We can help write any of these, with your guidance.

For now, each listing below uses the box art from one of its games as its image. Would you prefer a standard image for the meetup that we use every time instead? Catie is an artist and can make it. We also won’t be offended if you’d rather give us an AI mockup to use as a reference.

Games: Alien Languages (Acworth/Kennesaw)

About

This is a monthly gathering for anyone who might enjoy connecting with other members via gameplay experiences. THIS MONTH'S THEME: Overcoming language barriers, by intentionally building shared understanding. The games we'll be playing take different approaches to this, each using two groups. In one group, we remain as ourselves: humans who speak English. In the other, we roleplay as members of fictional ancient civilizations, or extraterrestrials. Some of our exploration will involve "Receptive Multilingualism", where people understand each others' languages, but only speak in their native tongue. GAMES ON THE TABLE: We'll play at least some of what is listed here. They're all quick to learn, and we'll teach you! No experience is necessary.
